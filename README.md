### 🏆 Featured projects

| Project | What I did | Tools | Link |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🎓 Predicting Student Dropout** | Built 12 XGBoost and neural network models on 25,000 student records across three stages of the student journey. Detection rose from 55 to 88 dropouts caught per 100 (AUC 0.885 → 0.992), but only once it was too late to act. Found and removed target leakage, and recommended excluding nationality for legal and ethical reasons. | `Python` `XGBoost` `TensorFlow/Keras` `scikit-learn` | [View repo](https://github.com/Arkarkhunx/student-dropout-prediction) |
| **📚 Book Sales Forecasting** | Forecast 32 weeks of UK book sales (Nielsen BookScan, 2001–2024) for two long-selling titles. Compared SARIMA, XGBoost, an LSTM tuned with KerasTuner, and SARIMA + LSTM hybrids; an optimised hybrid blend gave the lowest error. | `Python` `statsmodels` `pmdarima` `XGBoost` `TensorFlow/Keras` | [View repo](https://github.com/Arkarkhunx/book-sales-forecasting) |
| **🛒 Customer Segmentation** | Grouped 68,300 e-commerce customers (951,668 orders, 47 countries) into four segments for targeted marketing. Chose k with the elbow method, silhouette scores and a dendrogram, then visualised the segments with PCA and t-SNE. | `Python` `scikit-learn` `SciPy` `pandas` | [View repo](https://github.com/Arkarkhunx/customer-segmentation) |
